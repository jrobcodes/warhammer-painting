## Notes & observations

- The **50/50 Battle Mud + Flesh Wash** mix at step 7 is the functional replacement for the discontinued Cryptek Armourshade. Same role (warm gloss-ish tint over metals).
- The **50/50 Orc Flesh + Hazard Yellow Wash** mix at step 21 is how the eye/orb glow gets its yellow-green cast without a dedicated glow paint.
- Basic structure: base → all-over wash → re-layer up through highlights. Much more layer work than the original Citadel/Contrast recipe.
- 4-silver ladder (Doom Metal → Sir Coates → Mythril Blade → Platinum Crown) and 3-green ladder (Wyvern → Emerald → Talisman) give the updated scheme its more-layered, less-washy look.
- Duncan blacks the entire gauss flayer at step 14, so being sloppy with Sir Coates Silver on the flayer at step 3 is fine.
