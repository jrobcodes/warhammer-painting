## Key observations

- **Two 50/50 mixes** are core to the scheme:
  - Akhelian Green + Contrast Medium (body basecoat, thinned for smoothness)
  - Biel-tan + Cassandora Yellow (green glow tint — the signature Void Dragon look)
- **Iron Hands Steel** is used twice: first as a drybrush zenithal pass (step 2), then as a re-layer after the contrast coat (step 5). Different brush each time.
- **Canoptek Alloy**, not Necron Compound, is the bright brass at step 17. PDF checklist has it wrong.
- The **glow OSL** is a 6-layer stack across two passes: Corax White → Biel-tan+Cassandora tint → Warp Lightning crackle → Moot Green → Moon Dust → Matt White.
- Heavy use of Citadel Contrast + classic shades — structurally much more "wash-y" than the updated Warrior recipe.

## Why keeping an eye out for a TTC update is worth it

This recipe is structurally Contrast-heavy (Akhelian Green, Black Templar, Biel-tan, Cassandora). The TTC Warrior update showed Duncan moving away from single-coat Contrast toward layer ladders. If/when he updates the Void Dragon, expect: no Black Templar, no Cryptek Armourshade, no Warp Lightning, and more edge-highlight passes on the wings/rays like the Warrior's 4-silver ladder.
