## §1 Szarekhan Dynasty Necron Warrior (original Citadel recipe)

Source PDF: `Szarekhan-Dynasty-Necron-Warrior-Paint-List.pdf`
Applies to: Warriors, Immortals, Skorpekhs, Scarabs, Wraiths, Royal Warden, Plasmancer, Cryptothralls, Reanimator, Lokhust Heavies, Technomancer, Psychomancer, Triarch Stalker — rank-and-file metallic body.

1. Leadbelcher Spray (Citadel) — prime
2. **Black Templar** (Citadel Contrast) — all-over shadow ❌ *discontinued, but owned ×1*
3. Nuln Oil (Citadel) — deep recess wash
4. Runelord Brass (Citadel) — brass trim base
5. Canoptek Alloy (Citadel) — bright brass highlight
6. **Cryptek Armourshade** (Citadel) — gloss wash on metals ❌ *discontinued, but owned ×2 (18ml each)*
7. Stormhost Silver (Citadel) — edge highlights
8. Matt Black (Army Painter) — gun/weapon base — **use Abaddon Black (owned ×4)**
9. Caliban Green (Citadel) — gauss glow base
10. Warpstone Glow (Citadel) — gauss mid-layer
11. Moot Green (Citadel) — gauss highlight
12. Mechanicus Standard Grey (Citadel) — cable base
13. Matt White (Army Painter) — glow hotspot — **use White Scar (owned ×2)**
14. Biel-tan Green (Citadel) — green glow tint
15. Casandora Yellow (Citadel) — yellow-green tint mix partner
16. Moon Dust (Army Painter) — basing — **use Screaming Skull (owned)**

### Substitutions if you run out during the army

| Paint | Substitute | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cryptek Armourshade | **Reikland Fleshshade Gloss** (Citadel, still in range, ~$9) | Slightly cooler/redder tone. Test on one model before committing army-wide. |
| Black Templar | Basilicanum Grey (Citadel Contrast, **owned**) or AP Speedpaint Ash Barrens | Basilicanum gives a slightly less-black shadow |
| Matt Black (AP) | Abaddon Black (owned ×4) | 1:1 swap |
| Matt White (AP) | White Scar (owned ×2) | 1:1 swap |
| Moon Dust (AP) | Screaming Skull (owned) | 1:1 swap |

## Shopping recommendation for army consistency

**Buy now (total ~$15–30):**
1. **Reikland Fleshshade Gloss** (Citadel) — ~$9. The Cryptek Armourshade substitute. Test on one Warrior first; if it passes the eye test, use army-wide for repeatability, or keep in reserve as a transition paint when Cryptek runs out.
2. *(Optional)* **Retributor Armour** extra pot — ~$6. Insurance for the Lychguard gold-heavy treatment.
3. *(Optional)* **1 more Black Templar pot** from eBay — ~$15. Only if you can find a reliable seller; otherwise commit to Basilicanum Grey army-wide.

**Defer until needed:**
- Dechala Lilac — ~$5, buy when you sit down to paint the Void Dragon.

**Don't buy — substitute from existing inventory:**
- Matt Black AP → Abaddon Black (×4)
- Matt White AP → White Scar (×2)
- Moon Dust AP → Screaming Skull

**Decision to make now:** Do you want to commit to Cryptek Armourshade for the whole army (requires stockpiling ~1 more pot from eBay) or transition to Reikland Gloss from model 1 (fully repeatable forever)? The transition is recommended for consistency and future-proofing.
